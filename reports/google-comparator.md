# Google Routes API (Bus-Only) Comparator Report

## Methodological Notice: Comparator Only, Not Ground Truth

- **Google Maps Routes API is a live/future comparator, NOT ground truth.** It reflects Google's own bus schedule and traffic model, not observed GPS vehicle arrivals on Delhi roads. Disagreement between our model and Google does not imply either system is right or wrong; real ground-truth accuracy is measured strictly against observed October 2026 GPS stop passages in `reports/eval-full.md`.
- **October 2026 backtesting against Google is impossible.** Google Routes API `TRANSIT` does not support past departure times (verified during our feasibility probe: querying `2026-10-20T08:30:00+05:30` returns HTTP 200 with `routes: []`). All comparator queries therefore use **upcoming matching weekday/time slots** (`2026-11-24`, Tuesday, matching held-out Tuesday `2026-10-27` and the brief's Tuesday `08:30` example).
- **Which quantity is compared:** Our door-to-door `total_duration_s` includes walk-to-stop, the estimated first boarding wait, model-timed bus ride(s), joint-scenario transfer risk/wait, and egress walk. Google's `routes.duration` runs from leaving the origin, timed to its bus, to arriving at the destination, so it contains no first boarding wait. The like-for-like basis is therefore `our_duration_excluding_boarding_wait_s` = `our_total_duration_s` − `our_board_wait_s` (the median first boarding wait of our top option), compared as `diff_excluding_boarding_wait_s`. The door-to-door difference `diff_s` is kept as a separate row. Subtracting a median wait from a joint median total is an approximation, and our `[p10, p90]` window remains door-to-door. OTP and Google may also select different bus lines or transfer points. The CSV additionally keeps `diff_excl_board_s`, an older diagnostic built from the sum of marginal walk/ride/transfer leg medians. See `reports/google-comparator.csv`.
- **Only supported journeys are compared.** `GET /journeys` abstains unless some option has a ride with route-level observations (`observed_coverage > 0`), and the compared option must be one of those. Rows whose reported option has none are kept as `our_unsupported_option` (`our_option_supported = False`). Inputs frozen before per-ride coverage was recorded are classified from their timing sources and routes: only 717A rides (the one corridor with stop passages) and `model_live` rides carry observed coverage.

## Collection Contract & Call Accounting

- **Target weekday / date (IST):** `2026-11-24` (Tuesday; upcoming, zero October/past dates)
- **Time slots per OD pair:** morning_peak (08:30 IST), midday (14:00 IST), evening_peak (18:30 IST)
- **Corpus size:** 30 OD pairs × 3 time slots = **90 planned slots** (19 compared, 71 missing/error, 0 silently omitted)
- **Google request settings:** `travelMode=TRANSIT`, `transitPreferences.allowedTravelModes=['BUS']`, `languageCode="en"`, `computeAlternativeRoutes=true`, field-masked (`X-Goog-FieldMask`)
- **Strict bus-only post-filtering:** Every compared Google itinerary is verified to contain >=1 `TRANSIT` step with vehicle type `BUS` only (and `WALK` access/egress/transfers). **3** non-bus Google itinerary(ies) (e.g., Delhi Metro / `SUBWAY` or `RAIL`) were filtered out across the corpus.
- **Mission-wide Google call budget:** **92 / 300** (prior probe calls: 2, comparator network calls cached: 90, remaining budget: 208; cache hits on this run: 90)
- **Artifacts:** Report `reports/google-comparator.md`, CSV table `reports/google-comparator.csv`, request manifest `reports/google-manifest.json` (mirrored at `$DELHI_TRANSIT_DATA_DIR/google/manifest.json`), raw field-masked cache directory `$DELHI_TRANSIT_DATA_DIR/google/responses`.
